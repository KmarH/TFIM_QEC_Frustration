# Research Note 07 — Noise scaling / ZNE for the 6×6 triangular noise-scaling arm

Scope: literature for a planned (not run) on-device noise-scaling arm on Quantinuum H2:
36-spin frustrated triangular TFIM quench (J<0, h/|J| = 0.3, dt = 0.25, circuit-order Strang,
ZZPhase + Rx), diagonal dimer observables, Gauss-law post-selection, optional Iceberg, RC on every
ZZPhase. Questions: (1) foundational ZNE; (2) digital folding and its failure modes; (3) noise
amplification under twirling (PEA / sparse Pauli–Lindblad); (4) exactly what 2503.20870 did;
(5) ZNE + post-selection / symmetry, constrained observables, λ-dependence as a thermalization
diagnostic; (6) ZNE with error-detecting codes; (+) the HQC cost formula.

Method: every ID below was resolved on arxiv.org (abs page) on 2026-10-16; body-text claims were
read in the arXiv HTML or the PDF text layer where marked "full text". Nothing invented. Status
`verified` = ID resolves and the attached claim was checked at the stated level (abstract / full
text). Tags: [established] = stated by the source; [inferred] = my reading/derivation, not stated
by any source; [unverified] = not checked against a primary source. Every noisy curve in this
project remains a classical prediction under an ASSUMED noise model, never an H2 result.

## Answers per question

**Q1.** ZNE originates with 1612.02058 (Richardson) and 1611.09301 (amplify and extrapolate). 1805.04492 did the hardware demo. [established] That it used pulse stretching is [unverified].

**Q2.** 2005.10921 defines global/local folding, odd λ plus fractional partial folds, and the
extrapolants polynomial/Richardson/poly-exp/exp/adaptive-exp. It states the failure modes itself: coherent/systematic errors can be undone by G†, and SPAM is not scaled. Its exponential-in-λ law is proven only for global depolarizing noise. 2201.11792 adds that time-correlated noise breaks local folding. [established]

**Q3.** Twirling (1512.01098) makes each gate's noise a Pauli channel. PEA (Kim 2023; model from
2201.09866) then scales the learned Pauli–Lindblad rates by α, which is exact for the model. [established]
Inserting Paulis with probability (α−1)p_j gives α·p_j to first order, with O(p²) composition corrections. Folding instead adds *different* channels, from G† and from a second G. [inferred]

**Q4.** See the 2503.20870 row: Pauli insertion only before 2Q gates, a learned CB model averaged over ±θ, a two-point exponential fit, and for the triangular quench α = 4 nominal / α′ = 8.2 effective, with r = 0.5 and leakage post-selection. [established]

**Q5.** Combining extrapolation and symmetry verification has precedent in 2007.01265 and GUESS 2603.13060. Using λ-dependence to show that noise heats toward infinite T has precedent in 2407.04770 (small, IBM). No paper found does ZNE on dimer/Gauss-law observables at our scale. [established / gap]

**Q6.** No ZNE on Iceberg-encoded circuits was found. Logical-qubit ZNE exists only for distance-scaled or surface/repetition codes (2304.14985, 2501.09079), where the logical response is polynomial, not linear, in physical noise. [established; gap]

## Conflicts with "unitary folding of twirled ZZPhase scales the error rate linearly"

1. **Angle-dependent 2Q error** (2305.03828 Fig. 3; H2 docs). A folded ZZPhase(θ)·ZZPhase(−θ)·ZZPhase(θ) has error ≠ 3× unless the −θ gate error equals the +θ error, which was unmeasured. There is also a nonzero θ→0 floor. [established inputs; conclusion inferred]
2. **2503.20870 used no folding at all.** They learned the noise of the ±θ average and inserted Paulis. Their fit is exponential, not linear. [established]
3. **Non-Clifford conjugation.** Errors on the inner G† are conjugated by G = ZZ(θ). X/Y-type Paulis do not stay Pauli, so the folded channel is not α× the same Pauli channel. Only the first-order infidelity scales. Twirling the ZZ(θ) gate with a single-qubit X flips θ→−θ, which is why their model averages ±θ. [inferred]
4. **Unscaled components.** SPAM is not scaled by folding (2005.10921), and neither are leakage or memory/transport in proportion. Time-correlated (dephasing) noise breaks local folding (2201.11792). [established]
5. **Compiler cancellation.** The Quantinuum stack runs TKET at optimization level 2 by default, and a `no-opt` option exists (Azure page). [established] Adjacent G·G† pairs would likely be cancelled unless optimization is disabled. [inferred, not tested]
6. **Observable response is exponential or multi-exponential in λ, even when the error rate is linear** (2005.10921, 2007.01265, Kim 2023, 2407.04770). [established] Under post-selection, the accepted-shot error is non-linear in λ, because acceptance itself decays. [inferred]
