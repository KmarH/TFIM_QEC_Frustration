## 3. Findings

### Major (all fixed)
- **M1: the η ≈ 0.25 crossover and the ×0.60 ratio hold only on the 2×2 test lattice, but were stated generally.**
  - Cause: the 2×2 periodic torus is degenerate (B = N bonds).
  - In general the crossover solves (B + 0.2N)/(B + 2N) = 0.2 + 0.8η: η ≈ 0.36 on 2×3, ≈ 0.44 on large square tori, ≈ 0.55 on triangular tori. The ratio at η = 0.05 is 0.49 / 0.44 / 0.375 on those lattices.
  - Checked by the author against the repo's own Stim size data (0.375 vs 0.374).
  - This **predates this pass**: FLAG-0, `docs/encoded_tfim.md`, findings Q1 and review/08 all state the crossover without its lattice. It runs in the favourable direction; 2×2 is the most pessimistic case.
  - *Fix:* lattice-qualified in the report (abstract, §3.3 with the general formula, findings table), in `docs/encoded_tfim.md` (new paragraph), in the findings Q1 row, and in the FLAG-0 banner.
- **M2: the η prior was presented as "the prior" although the model's η is an *extra* ZZ fraction on top of the uniform 1/15.**
  - At η = 0.05 the model's total ZZ share is therefore 0.11, about twice the Fig. S3 read-off of 0.04–0.05.
  - The read-off channel is IZ+ZI-dominated, which puts Iceberg's undetectable share near 0.05–0.07 instead of the model's 0.24.
  - `docs/encoded_tfim.md` already said "conservative"; the report did not.
  - *Fix:* a "The prior is conservative" paragraph in §3.3.
  - The sub-agent's first-order estimate of ≈0.19× with the read-off spectrum is **not** quoted as a result: the model cannot run that channel (T-10).
- **M3: "3–5× lower acceptance" for the d ≥ 3 codes was stated without its regime.**
  - The data give 3–4× at p2 = 1e-3 on 2×3 and 1.4–1.6× at the H2 prior (the report's own Table 3).
  - *Fix:* the regime is now stated in the abstract, §4 and the findings table.
