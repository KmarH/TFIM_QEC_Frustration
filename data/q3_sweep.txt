# Q3 generalization study - noisy sweep over (N, h, lam). data/q3_sweep.txt
# CLASSICAL PREDICTION under an ASSUMED noise model (research/06 H2 prior, Pauli-trajectory MC). NOT an H2 / hardware result.
# Decision cards D1-D6 (project lead, 2026-10-22). Generalizes the single cell of
#   docs/q3_thermalization.md §9.2 (N = 12, h = 0.3, lam = 1; data/q3_noisy.txt).
# Model: J = -1, dt = 0.25; bare window = qdm_times / h; encoded window is
#   SURVIVAL-MATCHED per size (the largest window whose predicted detector survival
#   clears the floor; data/q3_scaling.txt), so encoded cells are compared at matched
#   survival, NOT at a matched window. Each cell states its own window.
# lam > 1 amplifies the 2q channel by Pauli insertion on BOTH arms (D6:
#   src/noise_scaling.run_bare_scaled / run_iceberg_scaled); 1q and SPAM stay at lam = 1.
# 'resolved' = the residual exceeds the mode's own mean batch SE. A residual at or below
#   the SE is NOT resolved: it is the statistical floor, not a win for either arm.
