# Interpretation (h=0, classical limit) — energy per bond is basis-independent:
#   square     E/bond at h=0 = -0.6667 over 24 bonds
#   triangular E/bond at h=0 = -0.3333 over 36 bonds (less negative -> more frustrated: a triangle satisfies only 1 of 3 bonds)
#   The h=0 ground state is degenerate on both lattices, so the per-vector |M_z| and
#   mean<ZZ> columns above are basis-dependent and not interpreted; energy/bond is the
#   honest frustration signature. The triangular defect density is ~0 in the ground
#   manifold (= defect-free Gauss-law subspace) and grows as (h/|J|)^2 off it
#   (data/gauss_law_scaling.txt). The square lattice has no dimer/defect description;
#   that observable is frustration-specific.
